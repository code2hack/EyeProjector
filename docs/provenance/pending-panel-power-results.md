# PAV-PWR-01: stock panel power on the locked Fold6

Worker: Worker-#78. Contract: [#78](https://github.com/code2hack/EyeBrowse/issues/78) / [PAV-PWR-1](panel-power-followup.md). PR target: `spike/fold6-rg-projector-av`. **Investigative candidate; independent review and acceptance pending.**

Stock panel OFF demonstrated a useful distinction from Android sleep: the active cover panel was visually dark, the existing keyguard stayed locked, Android remained Awake, and the direct RG projection continued with changing video and the owned decoded audio signal. Native tap/scroll effects were also demonstrated while locked with both compositor panel modes OFF. Explicit ON, Disconnect, receiver process loss and Host Stop restored software panel modes ON.

**The declared ten-minute operating case did not pass.** The unchanged RG video decoder stalled before collection completed. This is a bounded mechanism result with a retained endurance failure, not a production screen-off fix. Acoustic playback, continuous optical observation and a fresh capture session started with panels already blanked remain unqualified.
